Pro tips for evaluating models in your environment

  1. Evaluate in realistic production conditionsYour data, your prompts, your tools.
  2. Evals are very sensitive to the harnessSame answers, different scoring: a model moved up to 24 places.
  3. Be wary of small gainsWith 100 test cases, even a 10-point gain can be noise. You need about 20 points to trust it.
  4. Smaller can be betterPick on task fit, not size.
  5. Test the exact version you deployQuantized versions and different serving setups can behave differently on safety.
  6. Check leaderboards for production failure modesUse the BenchRisk questions. "Unknown" doesn't mean fine.
