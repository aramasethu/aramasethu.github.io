Can leaderboards predict production safety?

InfoSec World 2026, Gaylord Palms, Orlando.

Capability is roughly a property of a model. Safety is a property of a system in its deployment context. That gap is why a leaderboard position cannot tell you whether a model is safe for your use case, and what to build internally instead.

October 20–21, 2026

Serving Interpretability Probes on Open Models

PyTorch Conference North America 2026, San Jose. Poster, Community Expo.

Linear probes on the residual stream are cheap safety tooling, because the activations they read are already computed in the forward pass. Getting those activations out of a fast inference engine is the hard part: continuous batching, paged attention, CUDA graphs, tensor parallelism, and quantization each hide the internals a probe needs. I built an end-to-end probe on a small open model and measured the cost. Emitting hidden states and scoring on-device are free; reading each token's activation off-device in the decode loop costs about 24% of throughput. The poster walks through the measurement, locates the residual stream in the vLLM and SGLang forward paths, and outlines what per-layer, per-request activation access would take.
